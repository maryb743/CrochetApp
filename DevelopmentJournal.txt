2. DESIGN DECISIONS AND CHANGES
========================================

DESIGN D01 - APP CONCEPT

App:
Crochet project tracker.

Purpose:
Allow users to organise crochet projects, monitor progress, save inspiration and track completed rows.

Planned Screens:
- Home
- My Projects
- Project Details
- Inspiration
- Progress
- Profile

Project Details will be accessible through My Projects rather than the main navigation bar.

----------------------------------------
